# My Favorite Series

My favorite series is **The Boys**.

![The Boys]

## About the Series

The Boys is a superhero action series about a group of people who try to expose and stop corrupt superheroes. It shows that not all superheroes are good, and some use their powers for fame, money, and personal gain.

## Why I Like It

I like The Boys because it has exciting action scenes, interesting characters, and unexpected plot twists. The story is unique and different from other superhero series.

## My Favorite Character

**Billy Butcher** – I like his strong personality, determination, and courage in fighting against corrupt superheroes.
